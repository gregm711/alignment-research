# Pooling archived FluSight forecasts

## Protocol fixed before computing scores

Use CDC's versioned public weekly summaries from the 2024–25 and 2025–26 seasons, November 22 through May 31 by reference date, restricted to U.S. states and DC. Each stream is one state in one season. A team votes that its one-week-ahead median admission count will exceed the latest reported week strictly before the reference date available in the same issue's surveillance snapshot. Compare the target week's final archived count to that same threshold. Ties mean no increase. Predictions, threshold and truth all refer to weekly incident admissions. Omit state-weeks with no then-available reference, final outcome or fewer than four available team forecasts. Weekly issue files sometimes have gaps; the history advances by archived issue, and resets between seasons.

For each season use its first eight issues for calibration; with no holdout labels, select up to 16 models with the most nonmissing state-forecasts across calibration, at most one representative per named team (alphabetical ties), excluding FluSight's official baseline and ensemble. Freeze the roster. Generalization is to later issues. Report missing fractions. Fit simple smoothed model accuracy weights on calibration outcomes that were already present in the final calibration issue's surveillance snapshot. No tested outcomes train or update an arm.

Compare strict-majority vote, arithmetic mean and median of raw forecast counts, reliability-weighted votes and equal-vote redundancy correction. Estimate neighbors by agreement on at least six of the previous eight co-observed issues, at most one disagreement, with self-count one. Divide each available vote by its model's neighbor count. Compare freezing after calibration, fixed clocks at periods 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 issues, and the original reactive rule. Trigger the next refresh after two flags in four issues and a six-issue cooldown. A flag means no suspected group (degree at least 8) has available votes, or its absolute mean sign falls below 0.75. A missed forecast contributes no vote. This measures forecast similarity; it does not identify causal copying.

Prediction: the reactive arm gains at least one point in accuracy over majority and the reliability-weighted baseline, and appears on the accuracy vs. measured CPU energy frontier of the declared clocks. Report both seasons, eligible issue and location counts, disagreement and accuracy sliced on majority margin, post-calibration refreshes, received forecast signs and state-week weighted accuracy. Resample four-issue temporal blocks within season keeping all states together for paired descriptive 95% intervals. Measure batched process CPU energy and wall time on prepared inputs with the shared meter. Exclude forecast generation, acquisition and target verification. Count the eight calibration issues when reporting transmitted bits and initial similarity computations separately. No claim about public health forecasting performance beyond these selected summaries.

Sources: [CDC FluSight archive](https://github.com/cdcepi/FluSight-forecast-hub/tree/b5dd6a9bc9cf7b565c6c4fe992bd35a82fa2c2f3/weekly-summaries) and [CDC target-data documentation](https://github.com/cdcepi/FluSight-forecast-hub/blob/b5dd6a9bc9cf7b565c6c4fe992bd35a82fa2c2f3/target-data/README.md). The source revision pins the files. Downloaded records remain in the local cache.

Use named teams from the issue files and exclude all `FluSight-` hub-operated submissions, including its alternate ensembles. A reliability weight is the positive part of smoothed log odds, `max(0, log((correct+1)/(wrong+1)))`; uniform voting is the fallback if all available weights vanish. Inference starts from raw forecast medians and issue thresholds. Count one availability bit per roster member plus one vote bit per available forecast, with prearranged identities. Continuous-value controls need the forecast values themselves. No policy purchases an extra outcome check. Report the majority-margin diagnostic for a tie or absolute margin at most two votes. Signed votes and model-panel selection are inspectable. A paired temporal block interval spanning zero leaves the positive result unsettled.

A pre-scoring source-integrity assertion caught four folder dates that differ from the entire file's internal reference date and one missing issue snapshot. Two archived surveillance snapshots contained nonmissing values for the reference week or later. Exclude any issue with these conditions before selecting the first eight eligible dates. This ensures matching issue and target horizons and a strictly historical surveillance window. Rows were not relabeled to force coverage. The archive cannot establish the precise data vintage each team actually saw.
