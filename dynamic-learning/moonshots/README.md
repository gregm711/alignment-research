# Which unusual architectures earned a larger experiment?

We ran five proposed mechanisms alongside the existing open-weight teaching-cost experiment. Each line used a different task chosen to expose its mechanism. The scores across lines are not a leaderboard. All model fitting and evaluation ran locally. Individual reports contain the predictions recorded before scoring, controls, raw results and reproduction commands.

| Line | What we actually tested | What survived |
|---|---|---|
| Equilibrium learning | A local free/nudged learning rule with a numerical gradient reference on small convex chains. | The gradient converged as the nudge shrank. Relative error at the registered nudge size was 0.000123 against a 0.000100 target. Hardware energy and large nonlinear models remain open. [Study](equilibrium/README.md) |
| Predictive updates | Propagating residual errors across a chain after an observation changes, counting edge visits, routing and CPU time. | An isolated weakly coupled correction took 30 edge visits versus 1,778 for full relaxation. A conventional incremental solver took 12. For dense, strongly coupled changes, a fresh exact solve won. This tests local residual propagation within a known energy model. [Study](equilibrium/README.md) |
| Executable program memory | Inferring a scoped rule change, repairing cached consequences, and transferring a small program library to independent spreadsheet tasks. | Scoped bytecode and a compact rule table both recovered 99.17% of simulated policies. On BUSTLE, exhaustive search showed that the supplied grammar could express only 3/38 tasks. Reordering known programs could never solve the other 35. [Controlled edits](program-memory/README.md) · [External tasks](program-memory/external/README.md) |
| Competing hypotheses | Four explicit or learned explanations, a learned ordinary 32-bit state, and compressed evidence followed by a late query. | With explicit branches, choosing a check for its effect on the final decision beat choosing maximum disagreement by 5.45 percentage points. In a fresh matched learned comparison, slots scored 81.40%, a single learned state 81.77%, and a 27-bit exact record 90.74% when each chose its check. The exact decoder knew the likelihood and world space. [Study and learned comparison](multiworld/README.md) |
| Local prediction market | Four online forecasters with per-test wealth updated after verified results. Equivalent multiplicative weights, equal shares, retrieval, and simple failure history provided controls. | An apparent first-check gain on two ABB panels vanished on two new Jenkins projects. Market and equal weights each caught 5/110 failing Karaf builds and 38/72 Log4j builds with their first check. Shrinking each test's estimated risk toward the project's base rate caught 29 and 42. Local payment helped average forecast loss relative to equal weighting; it gave no external detection advantage. Module birth and long-horizon upstream credit were outside this pilot. [ABB](../remembering-tests/README.md) · [Apache replication](../remembering-tests/external/README.md) |
| Earlier learning-curve work | A 4,128-byte hybrid of retrieved experiences and learned decoder weights against both full-capacity parents. | Numeric tools needed 3.107 examples versus 3.233 with retrieval and 3.199 with weights. On text, hybrid and weights needed 3.225 and 3.228. A fixed split missed the registered 0.10-example gain across parents and domains. [Matched-byte study](../teaching-cost/hybrid/README.md) |

### The next research bet

Let a learner invent a short reusable operation **and keep a check that would tell it when that operation no longer applies**. For example, a system might turn several date conversions into a shared parser. The example that distinguishes day-first from month-first should travel with that parser. A new supplier can then be checked before old assumptions change its invoices. The spreadsheet result motivates invention because the existing grammar cannot express most tasks. The late-evidence result motivates preserving a discriminating check. Neither experiment tested this combined architecture.

The narrow architectural question is whether pairing a learned operation with a discriminating check pays for acquiring, storing and maintaining it when tasks recur and conventions change. Program library learning, experiment selection and equilibrium propagation all have established precedents. The scientific opportunity is to identify a region where this particular combination beats established alternatives at matched total cost, then explain the boundary where it fails. [DreamCoder](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.08381) · [Decision-relevant active learning](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~dgolovin/papers/nips10.pdf) · [Equilibrium propagation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.05179) · [Machine learning markets](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v15/storkey11a.html)

**Proposed 9/10 bar for a larger study.** Fix the thresholds before development. Use revision histories from independently maintained tools or data pipelines, with point-in-time documentation available to every baseline. Split by producer and time, remove repeated examples, and keep two independently sourced collections unopened until the method is frozen. Require enough eligible, genuinely changed tasks to resolve the chosen margin. Measure an upper bound on what the available operations and evidence can solve before incurring a training bill. Include off-the-shelf code generation with tests, simple retrieval plus a rule store, an established library-learning or synthesis method, and raw evidence replay. Charge everyone for learning the library, retained evidence, searches, checks and repairs. Estimate when the upfront bill breaks even.

My proposed positive criterion is **at least 20% fewer verified executions at matched held-out task success**, with positive paired intervals in both collections, and an upper confidence bound below two percentage points of excess regressions on previously working cases. Cross the operation and its saved check in a two-by-two ablation, swap checks between unrelated operations, and replay identical observations to separate asking from learning. Compare quality at multiple inference budgets. An independent reproduction and an operating region where a simple method wins would make the result more credible. A precise negative ruling out the practical gain across both collections would also settle a consequential question. These are target standards for a future result; no pilot here has met them.

### Rerun the portfolio

From the repository root, `python3 dynamic-learning/moonshots/parallel.py /tmp/architecture-rerun` runs the five independent lines with up to three local processes at once. Supply a new destination outside the research tree. Each line writes there, including its log. The original teaching-cost encoder and frozen settings are copied as inputs. First-time public dataset fetches are cached; the LRTS runner also reads pinned public build metadata. Use an interpreter with NumPy and Matplotlib, and add `--torch-python /path/to/python` if PyTorch and its matching NumPy are in a separate environment. Interpret timing from isolated runs. Concurrent runtimes include contention.
